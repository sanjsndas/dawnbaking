    The science of brioche: butter, eggs & patience

    By Margot Baker • 17 Apr 2026 • 19 min read

    Brioche is the ultimate enriched dough — soft, golden, and rich. But achieving that feathery crumb requires precision.

    • → Butter incorporation
    • → Egg temperature
    • → Bulk fermentation
    • → Shaping

    Butter: cold vs. softened

    For brioche, butter must be soft but not melted. We recommend European-style butter (82% fat). Incorporate in stages.

    “Brioche is 50% butter, 50% technique — and 100% patience.” — Margot

    Temperature control

    Keep your dough below 24°C (75°F) to prevent butter from melting into the flour.

    🍯 Baker’s tip: Chill your bowl and hook if the kitchen is warm.

    FAQs

    • Can I use salted butter? Yes, reduce added salt.
    • Why is my brioche dense? Under-kneaded or over-proofed.
