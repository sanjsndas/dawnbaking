Cookie policy

We use cookies like we use butter — sparingly, deliberately, and only to make things better.

Effective date: April 1, 2026  |  Last updated: April 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on Dawn Baking Corner, and how you can manage your preferences. We believe in transparent baking — and transparent browsing.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) to store information about your visit. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you use our site, and improve your experience. Think of them as a tiny pinch of salt — not the main ingredient, but they help everything work better.

How we use cookies

We use only essential and functional cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for third‑party marketing. Our cookies are divided into these categories:

TypePurposeExampleDuration
essential Necessary for the site to function (e.g., security, session management). session_id session
preference Remember your choices (e.g., dark mode, language, font size). theme_pref 1 year
analytics Anonymous, aggregated data to improve content (privacy‑first, no cross‑site tracking). _ga_* (anonymized) 14 months

🍪 We do not use tracking cookies for advertising, retargeting, or sharing data with third parties. Your cookie jar is yours alone.

Managing your cookie preferences

You can control and manage cookies in various ways. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. However, please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our site.

  • Browser settings — adjust your cookie preferences in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.).
  • Opt‑out tools — for analytics, we use a provider that offers an opt‑out mechanism (see below).
  • Our preference center — you can set your preferences via the cookie banner that appears when you first visit.

If you have any questions about managing cookies, contact us and we’ll be happy to help.

Third‑party cookies

We use a limited number of third‑party services that may place cookies:

  • Analytics — we use a privacy‑first analytics service that does not track individual users across websites. Their cookies are used solely for aggregated statistics.
  • Email sign‑up — if you subscribe to our newsletter, the mailing platform may place a cookie to remember your subscription status.

These third parties are contractually bound to process data only for the purposes we specify.

Your rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Know what cookies are being placed on your device.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where consent is required).
  • Request deletion of any data collected via cookies (subject to legal obligations).

To exercise these rights, please reach out to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. We’ll post the updated version here and update the effective date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, or if you’d like to exercise your rights, please contact us or email privacy@dawnbaking.corner.
