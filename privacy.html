Privacy policy

Your data is safe with us — we handle it like a sourdough starter: with care, respect, and transparency.

Effective date: April 1, 2026  |  Last updated: April 1, 2026

At Dawn Baking Corner, we believe in honest baking — and honest data handling. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy while you enjoy our recipes, articles, and community.

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal data to keep the site running and to connect with our audience. This includes:

  • Contact information — if you fill out our contact form, we receive your name, email, and message.
  • Comments and feedback — if you leave a comment (future feature), we store your name and email (optional).
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics (e.g., page views, referrers) to improve content. We use privacy-friendly tools.
  • Cookies — essential and preference cookies; see our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How we use your data

  • To respond to your inquiries and messages.
  • To send you occasional updates if you subscribe to our newsletter (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To analyze site performance and improve user experience.
  • To ensure the security and integrity of our website.

🔒 We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your trust is our most important ingredient.

3. Data storage & security

We store your data securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls. Our hosting provider is compliant with GDPR and CCPA regulations. We retain contact form messages for a reasonable period to manage ongoing conversations, after which they are deleted.

4. Your rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct any inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data (we’ll honour it unless we have a legal obligation to retain).
  • Objection — opt out of any non‑essential processing.

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us — we’ll respond within 30 days.

5. Third‑party services

We use a few carefully selected tools to enhance your experience:

  • Analytics — we use a privacy-first analytics service that does not track individual users across sites.
  • Email updates — if you subscribe, we use a reputable mailing platform; you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Social media — we provide links to our social profiles; those platforms have their own privacy policies.

We do not embed third‑party tracking scripts (like ads or retargeting) on our site.

6. Children's privacy

Our content is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or just want to talk about bread, our contact page is the best way to reach us. You can also email us directly at privacy@dawnbaking.corner.

🍞 This policy is crafted with care — just like our sourdough. Thank you for being part of our baking community.
